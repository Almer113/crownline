# Code of conduct

Crownline is about family trees of royal houses, and some of them are still disputed: successions, titles and claims that matter to living people. Disagreement about facts is welcome here, and is settled with sources. Disagreement about people is not.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all.
- Argue about sources and evidence, not about the person you disagree with.
- Respect the people in the charts and their descendants. Write about a disputed claim neutrally, and give each side's sources.
- Accept a maintainer's decision about what goes into a chart or the code, and ask for the reasons when they are not clear.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and slurs about anyone's background, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexuality or disability.
- Using a chart, an issue or a pull request to campaign for one side of a dynastic dispute.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeating any of these after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and wherever someone acts on the project's behalf.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers by writing to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If the problem involves a maintainer, report it to another one. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may warn or ban a contributor, temporarily or permanently. They act in proportion to what happened, and tell the person concerned why.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
